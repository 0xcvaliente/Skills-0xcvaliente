# Market, competitor, and demand research

Use for competitive intelligence, segments, market sizing, buying signals, or customer channels. Scope geography, use case, buyer, time window, and decision first.

## Map actual alternatives

Include direct products, other approaches to the same job, internal/manual work, agencies, and doing nothing. Capture target customer, job, value claim, price/billing basis, strengths, switching friction, and sourced gaps. Marketing claims are not independently verified performance.

Open official pricing pages. Record currency, region, monthly versus annual commitment, unit, minimum seats, included usage, stated taxes, and as-of date. Preserve “contact sales” or unavailable. Normalize only comparable packages and show arithmetic. Do not infer revenue from traffic, installs, funding, or followers.

## Trace demand signals

Search relevant public communities, reviews, support threads, GitHub issues, job postings, integrations, procurement material, and specialist publications. Use available connectors or public routes; do not require a particular scraper/token.

Retain URL, date, observable role, relevant wording or faithful paraphrase, problem context, workaround, and limitation. Deduplicate copied posts. Complaints can reveal a problem; they cannot establish budget, population size, or willingness to switch.

For GitHub, distinguish issues from pull requests and automated reports. Check whether a gap is resolved, planned, or intentional. Engagement helps prioritize but comments/reactions are not buyer counts. Describe small samples individually instead of forcing scored clusters.

For hiring signals, separate observation (“hiring an analyst”) from inference (“may need workflow tooling”). A job posting does not prove software budget or permission to contact someone.

## Find reachable channels

Recommend named communities, publications, partnerships, marketplaces, events, or search intents with observed buyer presence. Include URL and current rules/access route where relevant. Sourced membership and traffic estimates are optional and are not reachable-buyer counts.

For promising channels, give evidence of fit, specific entry tactic, useful content angle, posting constraints, effort/cost, and outcome to measure. Do not manufacture channels to meet a quota. State access and coverage limits; try another public route where useful.

## Size bottom-up

Use eligible buyers × feasible units per buyer × annual realized price, with sources or explicit assumptions for each input. Distinguish broad market, reachable initial segment, and capacity-limited near-term opportunity. Show ranges and sensitivity. Do not use an industry report total as obtainable revenue or assume one-percent capture.

Deliver a sourced comparison, gap hypothesis, access route, and next validation step. Keep observation, inference, and recommendation distinguishable. Refresh volatile facts for this decision while retaining dates for historical analysis.
