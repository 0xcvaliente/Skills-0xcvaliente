# SEO, AI visibility, and search discovery

Use for search strategy, technical SEO, content opportunities, comparison pages, programmatic SEO, or AI citation visibility. Verify current search-engine guidance when making platform-specific recommendations.

## Start with demand and intent

Identify the buyer's problem, category, evaluation terms, alternatives, and purchase/use stage. Use actual search data, site search, sales/support questions, current results, and competitor pages when available. Separate tool estimates from measured search-console data. A keyword's volume is not its qualified demand or revenue.

Map useful pages to distinct intents: problem solution, product/category, integration, comparison, use case, proof, or education. Prioritize achievable buyer value, differentiation, distribution, effort, and the conversion path. Do not default to mass blog production.

## Technical audit

Inspect current URLs and available crawl/search data. Check crawl and indexing access, status codes, canonicalization, redirects, duplicate content, sitemaps, robots directives, rendering, internal links, page titles and headings, mobile usability, and performance when relevant. Prioritize causes that prevent discovery or misrepresent the page before cosmetic metadata changes.

Provide evidence, affected URLs/template, causal explanation, exact fix, and verification method. Do not declare indexing or ranking success from a local code edit. Read back the rendered page and relevant live/search data when available.

Structured data should describe visible, accurate content using supported types. Validate syntax and current eligibility rules. Do not invent ratings, FAQs, availability, prices, or author expertise to populate markup.

## Content and programmatic pages

Create briefs and finished pages with unique utility, credible evidence, and a next step suited to the intent. Comparisons should be fair, current, and clear about billing and constraints. A missing competitor feature is not automatically a valuable opportunity.

For programmatic pages, require legitimate distinct intent and usable data for every generated page. Define dataset provenance, completeness, freshness, template, unique value, internal links, canonical/index policy, update rules, and small-batch verification. Empty location variants and swapped keywords do not create value. Scale after checking rendered examples and actual discovery/use.

## AI search and agent discovery

Make substantive information understandable and extractable: clear headings, direct answers, factual product/price information, meaningful tables, original evidence, and source dates. Measure visibility using documented query sets, provider/date/context, sampled responses, cited URLs, and referral/conversion data where available.

Treat provider behavior and published experiments as bounded observations. Do not promise citation rates or transplant unsupported uplift percentages. `llms.txt`, schema, special files, or bot access settings do not guarantee ranking or inclusion. Verify current official guidance and the site's business preferences before recommending crawler access; training access and search access may be different choices.

## Output and learning

Deliver the requested audit, page, content map, technical patch, or measured visibility report. Distinguish proposed work, implemented changes, crawl/index observations, and business outcomes. Compare relevant windows while considering seasonality, algorithm changes, site migrations, and tracking gaps. Use [conversion and measurement](conversion-analytics.md) for causal or attribution questions.

## Refresh and visibility measurement

For a requested ranking-content refresh, inspect current query intent and the
pages answering it before rewriting. Compare useful coverage and original
value, preserve working URLs and inbound heading anchors, and date changed
statistics. Match the reader's question rather than competitor word counts.

For AI visibility, sample repeatable buyer intents across problem, solution,
product comparison, and purchase/integration stages. Record citations separately
from product recommendations, and both separately from visits and conversions.
Fix a factual ambiguity on the owned source page when evidence supports it;
absence or one incorrect sampled response alone does not prove its cause.
