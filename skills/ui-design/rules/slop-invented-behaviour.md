## Ground product capabilities in the brief

Compare newly added settings, modes, roles, integrations, navigation, and market
claims with the brief, product documentation, and existing implementation. Report
an invented capability only with evidence that it contradicts or lacks support
in that source of truth. Without enough context, list the claim as unverified;
do not infer invention from unfamiliarity.

Existing product behavior and ordinary controls reasonably implied by the
requested feature are valid. Explicitly speculative prototypes may explore new
capabilities, but label those assumptions in the handoff. Remove unsupported
promises or record the unresolved product decision; do not invent features to
fill a layout. Numbers, logos, and testimonials stay with
`slop-unverifiable-proof`.
