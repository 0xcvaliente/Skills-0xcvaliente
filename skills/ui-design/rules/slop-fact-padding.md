## Remove sections that only repeat existing facts

Read each section against the information already supplied on the page. Flag
a card, FAQ, or section only when it adds no useful detail, explanation, proof,
or decision support. Repetition counts can locate candidates but cannot decide
the finding.

Terms repeated beside a decision, a feature demonstrated in a product preview,
and a deeper explanation are legitimate. Merge or remove empty restatements;
do not invent extra facts to meet a conventional page length. Keep the facts
where they best answer the reader's question.
