# Sales writing and evidence

## Build a specific, supportable message

Write for the reader's task and buying stage. Establish the category, then connect capability to mechanism to possible benefit within what is known. Quantitative benefits need measured data or a clearly labeled model; specificity is not permission to manufacture numbers.

Use language from supplied discovery/interviews, a clear author voice, concrete verbs, and technical words the audience needs. Preserve necessary qualifications. Avoid generic superiority, decorative urgency, dramatic fear, and a formulaic transformation story when evidence does not support it.

Describe a practical action: what can be requested, who receives it, and what happens next when known. Use confirmed destinations. Do not imply a free pilot, refund, instant setup, no-risk contract, or privacy guarantee to reduce hesitation unless the company authorized it.

## Working claim register

For a substantive document with multiple claims, track these fields outside final customer-facing copy:

| Field | What to record |
|---|---|
| Claim and location | Wording or identifier, page/section |
| Source | File/page/section, URL, or explicit company instruction |
| Status | Supported, company-stated, illustrative, modeled assumption, unresolved, or conflicting |
| Scope | Product/version, population, period, geography, units, and conditions |
| Publication wording | Narrowest accurate statement for this audience |
| Action | Keep, qualify, update, omit, or request a missing value |

Company statements are not automatically independent validation. Resolve conflicts using explicit corrections, authority, relevance, and date. If a material conflict cannot be resolved, ask once for the decisive information while progressing independent work.

Logo placement can imply endorsement, a current relationship, or deployment. Use only supported relationships and known publication permission. Team affiliations are not company customer proof. Distinguish synthetic screenshots and examples from real customer data and outcomes.

## Editing depth

For a targeted edit, apply the correction and verify meaning and local flow. For a substantive rewrite, inspect these dimensions as needed:

1. Meaning: preserve facts, conditions, and requested coverage.
2. Relevance: explain mechanism and consequence for this reader.
3. Evidence: narrow unsupported assertions and refresh changing facts.
4. Voice: retain formality and viewpoint while cutting empty phrasing.
5. Specificity: use real examples and values with units/context; unmeasured benefits stay qualitative.
6. Sequence: arrange the main point and support for the document's purpose.
7. Action: make the next step practical and proportionate to the earned commitment.

Recheck facts after strengthening promises. Do not mechanically remove qualifications or passive constructions. Do not add emotional pressure or offer conditions during proofreading. Provide alternatives only when useful or requested; make them materially distinct, choose the best fit, and explain tradeoffs briefly.

## Outcomes and financial models

Use measured baselines, prospect inputs, or conspicuously labeled assumptions. Distinguish time saved from cash savings unless a realizable cost change is established. Include implementation and recurring costs, currency, period, and timing when relevant. Define ROI; do not conflate revenue, gross margin, and net return. Zero investment or missing inputs means an undefined/incomplete result, not infinite benefit.

Model results are scenario estimates, not observed performance. Do not add a model to every brochure. Use Spreadsheets when an actual calculator is requested.

## Credibility when proof is limited

Use product detail, a labeled synthetic workflow, an evaluation plan, or an explanation of what remains to be tested. Change the proof format rather than inventing content for a template slot. Describe supported security, regulatory, and certification status accurately; do not convert aspirations or vendor statements into certified compliance.

## Review output

Locate each finding, explain the reader impact, and provide a concrete repair. Prioritize wrong/overstated facts, missing required content, confusing actions, and unreadable presentation. Avoid cosmetic preferences unrelated to the brief. Simulated buyer/editor viewpoints are internal prompts, not actual independent expert reviews.
