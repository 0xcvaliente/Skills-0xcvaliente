Frosted glass

Blur + saturate + a tint that is opaque enough to keep this text at 4.5:1 over a pure white or pure black backdrop.

Scroll so the striped and coloured blocks pass under the header and the floating toolbar. The header blur fades out with a mask (a scroll edge effect) instead of ending in a hard divider. In Chromium the toolbar bends the content behind its rim through an SVG displacement map; in Safari and Firefox it falls back to a light blur with the same rim lighting.

Only the primary action is tinted. Settings are content, so they sit on a solid surface. The menu is a sibling of the toolbar, not a child, so its own backdrop blur still sees the page.
